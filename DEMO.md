# Demo script: 30-minute boardroom slot

**Before you go in (T-10 min):** open the URL so the server wakes (free tier). Sign in. Settings → **Run checks**: all green.
Close any old cases. Have one rehearsed live case ready (Download JSON from rehearsal; **Import a case** if it was lost).

**0:00 Set-up (1 min).** The intro card: an AI agent that does the legwork while you make the calls. Top strip = the
12 steps (blue = agent, orange = you). Right pane = what happens behind each screen. Click **Start the walkthrough**.

**Sample 1, credit card launch, India (about 12 min). Try a sample. It runs in Demo data mode: stored answers, said openly.**
1. *The ask*: the client's words kept verbatim; files already received; private numbers that never leave. **Check the ask.**
2. *Question*: the belief becomes a claim to test, not a fact. **Confirm question.**
3. *Targets*: each idea gets a target **before** any evidence is read; H1 has a fetched benchmark with its quality and date. Lock.
4. *Plan*: what the client's files already answer; **Requests** tab: the client is asked only for the gap; names removed. Tick, **Mark as sent**.
5–6. Runs alone: 25 collected, 16 separate sources; 6 copies of one forecast count once (point at Merged duplicates).
7. *Review*: 6 passed the 9-test check **but you still see their sources**; 10 need your call (open E7: the calculated
   growth rate and its formula). Quick double-check on E14. This is the human gate. **Run tests.**
8. *Results*: point at the **target-versus-evidence bar**. H1 Not supported, Strong: the 30% belief fails. H2 Not enough evidence.
9. *Fill the gaps*: one narrow question drafted; you send it yourself; **Use the sample reply**; 2,400 ÷ 110 = 21.8 months, formula confirmed by you. **Test again**: H2 Supported.
10. *The answer*: Not achievable, by a rule locked at step 3. Every sentence cites its evidence.
11. *What if*: move **Months needed to build** to 9: H4 flips. "Locked plan unchanged."
12. *Your conclusion*: the box is **empty**. The agent never writes the recommendation. Print, Markdown, JSON.

**Shortcut if time is short:** Settings → **Skip to step** 7, then 8, then 11 (it replays the real steps; it never skips a gate).

**Generality (2 min).** Home → Try a sample → **New hospital, Nakuru**: a different country, industry and measures, with the same tool and no code change.

**Live case (8 min).** Open the rehearsed live case, or start one: paste a real ask, Check the ask, Lock targets, Mark as sent.
In step 6 open **Details**: how many quotes could not be found in their source (they never count). Settings shows the cost (about 0.50 USD).

**If the AI or search is slow on the day:** say so, open the case's red banner → **Use demo data** (or a sample case). Keep going.

**Close (2 min).** The roadmap button (top right): implementation and scalability. Questions.
