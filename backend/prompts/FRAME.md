Frame the client's ask so it can be tested.
- client_belief: what the client believes, stated as one claim to test (for example "The market grows about 30% a year."). Keep their numbers. It is a claim, not a fact.
- decision: the decision the client faces, as one short question.
- case_measure_name: one measure for comparing market-size numbers in this case (for example "Hospital beds in use, county level"). Be precise about what is counted.
- case_measure_definition: one sentence that defines exactly what that measure counts, where and how.
Use the figures from the client's files if they help. Do not answer the question.
